## Unit 6: Gender Equality

- **equality (n) /iˈkwɒləti/**: sự bình đẳng.
  - _Collocation_: gender equality, fight for equality.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many countries are still working towards gender equality in the workplace.
- **gender (n) /ˈdʒendə/**: giới, giới tính.
  - _Collocation_: gender roles, gender gap.
  - _Ví dụ_: In our class, everyone has the same rights regardless of gender.
- **discrimination (n) /dɪˌskrɪmɪˈneɪʃn/**: sự phân biệt đối xử.
  - _Collocation_: gender discrimination, face discrimination.
  - _Ví dụ_: She reported the discrimination she faced when applying for the job.
- **equal (adj) /ˈiːkwəl/**: ngang bằng, bình đẳng.
  - _Collocation_: equal pay, equal opportunities.
  - _Ví dụ_: Men and women should receive equal pay for the same work.
- **opportunity (n) /ˌɒpəˈtjuːnəti/**: cơ hội.
  - _Collocation_: have the opportunity to do something.
  - _Ví dụ_: Girls should have the same opportunity to study science as boys.
- **pursue (v) /pəˈsjuː/**: theo đuổi.
  - _Collocation_: pursue a career, pursue a dream.
  - _Ví dụ_: My sister wants to pursue a career in engineering.
- **career (n) /kəˈrɪə/**: sự nghiệp.
  - _Collocation_: build a career, career path.
  - _Ví dụ_: She gave up a promising career to look after her family.
- **responsibility (n) /rɪˌspɒnsəˈbɪləti/**: trách nhiệm.
  - _Collocation_: share the responsibility, take responsibility for.
  - _Ví dụ_: Parents should share the responsibility of raising their children.
- **chore (n) /tʃɔː/**: việc vặt trong nhà.
  - _Collocation_: household chores, do the chores.
  - _Ví dụ_: My brother and I do the household chores together every weekend.
- **breadwinner (n) /ˈbredwɪnə/**: trụ cột kinh tế trong gia đình.
  - _Collocation_: the main breadwinner.
  - _Ví dụ_: In many families today, the mother is the main breadwinner.
- **treat (v) /triːt/**: đối xử.
  - _Collocation_: treat someone equally, treat someone with respect.
  - _Ví dụ_: Teachers should treat all students equally.
- **attitude (n) /ˈætɪtjuːd/**: thái độ.
  - _Collocation_: change one's attitude, a positive attitude.
  - _Ví dụ_: People's attitudes towards women in leadership have changed a lot.
- **prejudice (n) /ˈpredʒudɪs/**: định kiến.
  - _Collocation_: prejudice against someone.
  - _Ví dụ_: There is still prejudice against female drivers in some places.
- **stereotype (n) /ˈsteriətaɪp/**: khuôn mẫu, quan niệm rập khuôn.
  - _Collocation_: gender stereotypes, break a stereotype.
  - _Ví dụ_: The idea that boys cannot cook is just a stereotype.
- **workforce (n) /ˈwɜːkfɔːs/**: lực lượng lao động.
  - _Collocation_: enter the workforce, women in the workforce.
  - _Ví dụ_: Women now make up nearly half of the workforce in our country.
- **promotion (n) /prəˈməʊʃn/**: sự thăng chức.
  - _Collocation_: get a promotion, be considered for promotion.
  - _Ví dụ_: She worked hard and finally got a promotion last month.
- **eliminate (v) /ɪˈlɪmɪneɪt/**: loại bỏ, xoá bỏ.
  - _Collocation_: eliminate discrimination, eliminate inequality.
  - _Ví dụ_: New laws aim to eliminate discrimination in hiring.
- **female (adj) /ˈfiːmeɪl/**: thuộc nữ giới, nữ.
  - _Collocation_: female students, female leaders.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our school has three female science teachers.
- **domestic violence (phr) /dəˌmestɪk ˈvaɪələns/**: bạo lực gia đình.
  - _Collocation_: suffer from domestic violence.
  - _Ví dụ_: The organisation helps women who suffer from domestic violence.
- **give up (phr) /ˌɡɪv ˈʌp/**: từ bỏ.
  - _Collocation_: give up a job, give up studying.
  - _Ví dụ_: No girl should have to give up school because of her gender.
- **household (n) /ˈhaʊshəʊld/**: hộ gia đình.
  - _Collocation_: head of the household, household income.
  - _Ví dụ_: In this village, women manage the money in most households.
- **role (n) /rəʊl/**: vai trò.
  - _Collocation_: play an important role, traditional roles.
  - _Ví dụ_: Men and women play equally important roles in the family.
