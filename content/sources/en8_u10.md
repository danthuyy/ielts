## Unit 10: Communication in the Future

- **communicate (v) /kəˈmjuːnɪkeɪt/**: giao tiếp, liên lạc.
  - _Collocation_: communicate with someone.
  - _Ví dụ_: I communicate with my cousin in Japan every weekend.
- **communication (n) /kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/**: sự giao tiếp, truyền thông.
  - _Collocation_: a means of communication.
  - _Ví dụ_: Email is still an important means of communication.
- **face-to-face (adj) /ˌfeɪs tə ˈfeɪs/**: trực tiếp, mặt đối mặt.
  - _Collocation_: a face-to-face conversation.
  - _Ví dụ_: A face-to-face conversation feels warmer than a text.
- **video call (n) /ˈvɪdiəʊ kɔːl/**: cuộc gọi video.
  - _Collocation_: make a video call.
  - _Ví dụ_: We make a video call to our grandparents every Sunday.
- **instant messaging (phr) /ˌɪnstənt ˈmesɪdʒɪŋ/**: nhắn tin tức thời.
  - _Ví dụ_: Instant messaging lets us send photos in a second.
- **social media (phr) /ˌsəʊʃl ˈmiːdiə/**: mạng xã hội.
  - _Collocation_: use social media.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many students use social media to share their school news.
- **device (n) /dɪˈvaɪs/**: thiết bị.
  - _Collocation_: a smart device.
  - _Ví dụ_: This smart device can read your messages aloud.
- **hologram (n) /ˈhɒləɡræm/**: ảnh nổi ba chiều.
  - _Ví dụ_: In the future, we may talk to a hologram of our friend.
- **virtual reality (phr) /ˌvɜːtʃuəl riˈæləti/**: thực tế ảo.
  - _Ví dụ_: With virtual reality, we can visit a museum without leaving home.
- **telepathy (n) /təˈlepəθi/**: thần giao cách cảm.
  - _Ví dụ_: Some people believe we will use telepathy to talk in the future.
- **wearable (adj) /ˈweərəbl/**: có thể đeo trên người.
  - _Collocation_: wearable technology.
  - _Ví dụ_: Wearable technology can check your heart while you run.
- **smart watch (n) /ˈsmɑːt wɒtʃ/**: đồng hồ thông minh.
  - _Ví dụ_: My brother answers calls on his smart watch.
- **translate (v) /trænzˈleɪt/**: dịch.
  - _Collocation_: translate into Vietnamese.
  - _Ví dụ_: This app can translate English into Vietnamese at once.
- **voice recognition (phr) /ˈvɔɪs ˌrekəɡnɪʃn/**: nhận diện giọng nói.
  - _Ví dụ_: Voice recognition helps people write messages without typing.
- **keep in touch (phr) /ˌkiːp ɪn ˈtʌtʃ/**: giữ liên lạc.
  - _Ví dụ_: We keep in touch with our old teacher by email.
- **network (n) /ˈnetwɜːk/**: mạng lưới.
  - _Collocation_: a mobile network.
  - _Ví dụ_: The mobile network is very weak in the mountains.
- **wireless (adj) /ˈwaɪələs/**: không dây.
  - _Collocation_: a wireless connection.
  - _Ví dụ_: The library has a free wireless connection.
- **emoji (n) /ɪˈməʊdʒi/**: biểu tượng cảm xúc.
  - _Ví dụ_: She ended her message with a smiling emoji.
- **connect (v) /kəˈnekt/**: kết nối.
  - _Collocation_: connect to the internet.
  - _Ví dụ_: You can connect to the internet from any classroom.
- **app (n) /æp/**: ứng dụng.
  - _Collocation_: download an app.
  - _Ví dụ_: I downloaded a new app to learn English words.
- **text (v) /tekst/**: nhắn tin.
  - _Ví dụ_: Text me when you get home, please.
