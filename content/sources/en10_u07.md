## Unit 7: Viet Nam and International Organisations

- **organisation (n) /ˌɔːɡənaɪˈzeɪʃn/**: tổ chức.
  - _Collocation_: an international organisation, join an organisation.
  - _Ví dụ_: Viet Nam is a member of many international organisations.
- **international (adj) /ˌɪntəˈnæʃnəl/**: thuộc quốc tế.
  - _Collocation_: international cooperation, international law.
  - _Ví dụ_: The country takes part in many international events every year.
- **member (n) /ˈmembə/**: thành viên.
  - _Collocation_: become a member of, a member state.
  - _Ví dụ_: Viet Nam became a member of ASEAN in 1995.
- **join (v) /dʒɔɪn/**: gia nhập, tham gia.
  - _Collocation_: join an organisation, join a campaign.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our school joined a campaign to help flood victims.
- **cooperation (n) /kəʊˌɒpəˈreɪʃn/**: sự hợp tác.
  - _Collocation_: economic cooperation, close cooperation.
  - _Ví dụ_: Close cooperation between countries helps solve global problems.
- **peace (n) /piːs/**: hoà bình.
  - _Collocation_: maintain peace, world peace.
  - _Ví dụ_: The United Nations works to maintain peace around the world.
- **security (n) /sɪˈkjʊərəti/**: an ninh.
  - _Collocation_: peace and security, food security.
  - _Ví dụ_: The organisation aims to protect peace and security in the region.
- **humanitarian (adj) /hjuːˌmænɪˈteəriən/**: mang tính nhân đạo.
  - _Collocation_: humanitarian aid, humanitarian work.
  - _Ví dụ_: They sent humanitarian aid to the areas hit by the earthquake.
- **refugee (n) /ˌrefjuˈdʒiː/**: người tị nạn.
  - _Collocation_: help refugees, refugee camp.
  - _Ví dụ_: The UNHCR provides food and shelter for refugees.
- **aid (n) /eɪd/**: viện trợ.
  - _Collocation_: provide aid, medical aid.
  - _Ví dụ_: Several countries provided aid after the flood.
- **promote (v) /prəˈməʊt/**: thúc đẩy, đẩy mạnh.
  - _Collocation_: promote peace, promote education.
  - _Ví dụ_: UNESCO promotes education and culture in many countries.
- **sustainable (adj) /səˈsteɪnəbl/**: bền vững.
  - _Collocation_: sustainable development, sustainable growth.
  - _Ví dụ_: Sustainable development is one of the UN's main goals.
- **development (n) /dɪˈveləpmənt/**: sự phát triển.
  - _Collocation_: economic development, development programme.
  - _Ví dụ_: The programme supports the development of poor rural areas.
- **summit (n) /ˈsʌmɪt/**: hội nghị thượng đỉnh.
  - _Collocation_: attend a summit, hold a summit.
  - _Ví dụ_: Leaders from ten countries attended the ASEAN summit in Ha Noi.
- **treaty (n) /ˈtriːti/**: hiệp ước.
  - _Collocation_: sign a treaty, a peace treaty.
  - _Ví dụ_: More than a hundred nations have signed the treaty.
- **non-governmental (adj) /ˌnɒn ɡʌvnˈmentl/**: phi chính phủ.
  - _Collocation_: a non-governmental organisation (NGO).
  - _Ví dụ_: Many non-governmental organisations work with children in poor areas.
- **volunteer (n) /ˌvɒlənˈtɪə/**: tình nguyện viên.
  - _Collocation_: work as a volunteer, a group of volunteers.
  - _Ví dụ_: Hundreds of volunteers helped to build the new school.
- **crisis (n) /ˈkraɪsɪs/**: khủng hoảng.
  - _Collocation_: a health crisis, deal with a crisis.
  - _Ví dụ_: The organisation sent doctors during the health crisis.
- **contribute (v) /kənˈtrɪbjuːt/**: đóng góp.
  - _Collocation_: contribute to something, contribute money.
  - _Ví dụ_: Viet Nam has contributed to peacekeeping missions in Africa.
- **charity (n) /ˈtʃærəti/**: tổ chức từ thiện.
  - _Collocation_: a charity organisation, give to charity.
  - _Ví dụ_: The charity has helped thousands of poor children go to school.
- **global (adj) /ˈɡləʊbl/**: toàn cầu.
  - _Collocation_: global issues, a global effort.
  - _Ví dụ_: Hunger and disease are serious global issues.
- **mission (n) /ˈmɪʃn/**: sứ mệnh, nhiệm vụ.
  - _Collocation_: a peacekeeping mission, carry out a mission.
  - _Ví dụ_: Vietnamese soldiers took part in a peacekeeping mission in South Sudan.
