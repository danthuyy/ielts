## Unit 9: Natural Disasters

- **disaster (n) /dɪˈzɑːstə(r)/**: thảm hoạ.
  - _Collocation_: a natural disaster.
  - _Ví dụ_: A natural disaster can destroy a whole village in minutes.
- **earthquake (n) /ˈɜːθkweɪk/**: trận động đất.
  - _Collocation_: a strong earthquake.
  - _Ví dụ_: A strong earthquake shook the city last night.
- **flood (n) /flʌd/**: trận lũ lụt.
  - _Collocation_: a serious flood.
  - _Ví dụ_: The flood covered all the rice fields in our village.
- **drought (n) /draʊt/**: hạn hán.
  - _Collocation_: a long drought.
  - _Ví dụ_: The long drought killed most of the crops.
- **tsunami (n) /tsuːˈnɑːmi/**: sóng thần.
  - _Ví dụ_: The tsunami hit the coast an hour after the earthquake.
- **volcano (n) /vɒlˈkeɪnəʊ/**: núi lửa.
  - _Collocation_: an active volcano.
  - _Ví dụ_: There is an active volcano on that island.
- **eruption (n) /ɪˈrʌpʃn/**: sự phun trào.
  - _Ví dụ_: The eruption covered the fields with grey ash.
- **landslide (n) /ˈlændslaɪd/**: sạt lở đất.
  - _Ví dụ_: Heavy rain caused a landslide on the mountain road.
- **storm (n) /stɔːm/**: cơn bão.
  - _Collocation_: a heavy storm.
  - _Ví dụ_: The storm broke many windows in our street.
- **typhoon (n) /taɪˈfuːn/**: bão nhiệt đới.
  - _Ví dụ_: The typhoon is moving towards the central provinces.
- **wildfire (n) /ˈwaɪldfaɪə(r)/**: cháy rừng.
  - _Ví dụ_: A wildfire burnt hundreds of trees in the forest.
- **victim (n) /ˈvɪktɪm/**: nạn nhân.
  - _Collocation_: help the victims.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our school collected clothes to help the flood victims.
- **survivor (n) /səˈvaɪvə(r)/**: người sống sót.
  - _Ví dụ_: The survivors were taken to a safe place.
- **evacuate (v) /ɪˈvækjueɪt/**: sơ tán.
  - _Ví dụ_: The police evacuated the village before the storm arrived.
- **rescue (v) /ˈreskjuː/**: cứu hộ, giải cứu.
  - _Collocation_: rescue people.
  - _Ví dụ_: Soldiers rescued ten people from the flooded houses.
- **shelter (n) /ˈʃeltə(r)/**: nơi trú ẩn.
  - _Collocation_: take shelter.
  - _Ví dụ_: The families took shelter in the school hall.
- **collapse (v) /kəˈlæps/**: sụp đổ.
  - _Ví dụ_: The old bridge collapsed during the earthquake.
- **destroy (v) /dɪˈstrɔɪ/**: phá huỷ.
  - _Ví dụ_: The storm destroyed hundreds of homes.
- **warning (n) /ˈwɔːnɪŋ/**: lời cảnh báo.
  - _Collocation_: an early warning.
  - _Ví dụ_: People left the beach after the early warning on TV.
- **emergency (n) /ɪˈmɜːdʒənsi/**: tình huống khẩn cấp.
  - _Collocation_: in an emergency.
  - _Ví dụ_: In an emergency, call this number immediately.
- **first-aid kit (phr) /ˌfɜːst ˈeɪd kɪt/**: bộ dụng cụ sơ cứu.
  - _Ví dụ_: Every family should keep a first-aid kit at home.
