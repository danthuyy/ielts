## Unit 12: Life on Other Planets

- **planet (n) /ˈplænɪt/**: hành tinh.
  - _Collocation_: a distant planet.
  - _Ví dụ_: Scientists have found a distant planet with water.
- **alien (n) /ˈeɪliən/**: người ngoài hành tinh.
  - _Ví dụ_: In the film, a friendly alien lands in a small town.
- **UFO (n) /ˌjuː ef ˈəʊ/**: vật thể bay không xác định.
  - _Collocation_: see a UFO.
  - _Ví dụ_: He said he saw a UFO above the field last night.
- **spacecraft (n) /ˈspeɪskrɑːft/**: tàu vũ trụ.
  - _Ví dụ_: The spacecraft sent back photos of the red desert.
- **astronaut (n) /ˈæstrənɔːt/**: phi hành gia.
  - _Ví dụ_: The astronaut stayed on the space station for six months.
- **galaxy (n) /ˈɡæləksi/**: thiên hà.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our galaxy contains billions of stars.
- **universe (n) /ˈjuːnɪvɜːs/**: vũ trụ.
  - _Ví dụ_: The universe is much bigger than we can imagine.
- **solar system (phr) /ˈsəʊlə ˌsɪstəm/**: hệ mặt trời.
  - _Ví dụ_: There are eight planets in our solar system.
- **Mars (n) /mɑːz/**: sao Hoả.
  - _Collocation_: travel to Mars.
  - _Ví dụ_: Some people hope to travel to Mars in the future.
- **orbit (v) /ˈɔːbɪt/**: quay quanh quỹ đạo.
  - _Ví dụ_: The Moon orbits the Earth once a month.
- **gravity (n) /ˈɡrævəti/**: trọng lực.
  - _Collocation_: low gravity.
  - _Ví dụ_: Because of low gravity, you can jump very high on the Moon.
- **telescope (n) /ˈtelɪskəʊp/**: kính viễn vọng.
  - _Collocation_: look through a telescope.
  - _Ví dụ_: We looked at Saturn through a telescope.
- **extraterrestrial (adj) /ˌekstrətəˈrestriəl/**: ngoài Trái Đất.
  - _Collocation_: extraterrestrial life.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many scientists are still looking for extraterrestrial life.
- **life form (phr) /ˈlaɪf fɔːm/**: dạng sống, sinh vật.
  - _Ví dụ_: A simple life form may live under the ice.
- **exist (v) /ɪɡˈzɪst/**: tồn tại.
  - _Ví dụ_: Do you believe that aliens really exist?
- **creature (n) /ˈkriːtʃə(r)/**: sinh vật.
  - _Collocation_: a strange creature.
  - _Ví dụ_: The boy drew a strange creature with three eyes.
- **explore (v) /ɪkˈsplɔː(r)/**: khám phá, thám hiểm.
  - _Collocation_: explore space.
  - _Ví dụ_: Humans have explored only a tiny part of space.
- **launch (v) /lɔːntʃ/**: phóng (tàu vũ trụ).
  - _Ví dụ_: They will launch a new rocket next month.
- **land (v) /lænd/**: hạ cánh, đáp xuống.
  - _Collocation_: land on the Moon.
  - _Ví dụ_: The first men landed on the Moon in 1969.
- **atmosphere (n) /ˈætməsfɪə(r)/**: bầu khí quyển.
  - _Ví dụ_: The atmosphere of Mars is very thin and cold.
- **surface (n) /ˈsɜːfɪs/**: bề mặt.
  - _Collocation_: the surface of the planet.
  - _Ví dụ_: The surface of the planet is covered with red dust.
- **oxygen (n) /ˈɒksɪdʒən/**: khí ô-xy.
  - _Ví dụ_: Without oxygen, humans cannot live on other planets.
