## Unit 4: Ethnic Groups of Viet Nam

- **ethnic group (n) /ˈeθnɪk ɡruːp/**: dân tộc, nhóm dân tộc.
  - _Collocation_: an ethnic minority group.
  - _Ví dụ_: There are fifty-four ethnic groups in Viet Nam.
- **minority (n) /maɪˈnɒrəti/**: (nhóm) thiểu số.
  - _Collocation_: ethnic minority people.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many ethnic minority people live in the northern mountains.
- **majority (n) /məˈdʒɒrəti/**: (nhóm) đa số.
  - _Collocation_: the majority of the population.
  - _Ví dụ_: The Kinh people make up the majority of the population.
- **costume (n) /ˈkɒstjuːm/**: trang phục (truyền thống).
  - _Collocation_: a traditional costume.
  - _Ví dụ_: The girls wear colourful traditional costumes at the festival.
- **stilt house (n) /ˈstɪlt haʊs/**: nhà sàn.
  - _Collocation_: live in a stilt house.
  - _Ví dụ_: The Thai people live in stilt houses made of wood.
- **terraced field (n) /ˈterəst fiːld/**: ruộng bậc thang.
  - _Collocation_: grow rice on terraced fields.
  - _Ví dụ_: The terraced fields in Sa Pa turn golden in September.
- **heritage (n) /ˈherɪtɪdʒ/**: di sản.
  - _Collocation_: cultural heritage.
  - _Ví dụ_: These old songs are part of our cultural heritage.
- **unique (adj) /juˈniːk/**: độc đáo, đặc biệt.
  - _Collocation_: a unique culture.
  - _Ví dụ_: Each ethnic group has its own unique culture.
- **weave (v) /wiːv/**: dệt.
  - _Collocation_: weave brocade, weave cloth.
  - _Ví dụ_: Hmong women weave beautiful cloth by hand.
- **brocade (n) /brəˈkeɪd/**: vải thổ cẩm.
  - _Collocation_: a brocade dress.
  - _Ví dụ_: She bought a brocade bag at the mountain market.
- **custom (n) /ˈkʌstəm/**: phong tục.
  - _Collocation_: a local custom.
  - _Ví dụ_: Each ethnic group has its own interesting customs.
- **ritual (n) /ˈrɪtʃuəl/**: nghi lễ.
  - _Collocation_: perform a ritual.
  - _Ví dụ_: The village performs a ritual to pray for good crops.
- **musical instrument (n) /ˈmjuːzɪkl ˈɪnstrəmənt/**: nhạc cụ.
  - _Collocation_: play a musical instrument.
  - _Ví dụ_: The gong is a famous musical instrument of the Central Highlands.
- **gong (n) /ɡɒŋ/**: cồng, chiêng.
  - _Collocation_: beat a gong.
  - _Ví dụ_: The men beat gongs during the village festival.
- **speciality (n) /ˌspeʃiˈæləti/**: đặc sản.
  - _Collocation_: a local speciality.
  - _Ví dụ_: Five-colour sticky rice is a speciality of this region.
- **highland (n) /ˈhaɪlənd/**: vùng cao, cao nguyên.
  - _Collocation_: the Central Highlands.
  - _Ví dụ_: Coffee grows very well in the Central Highlands.
- **population (n) /ˌpɒpjuˈleɪʃn/**: dân số.
  - _Collocation_: a small population.
  - _Ví dụ_: This ethnic group has a population of about ten thousand.
- **diverse (adj) /daɪˈvɜːs/**: đa dạng.
  - _Collocation_: a diverse culture.
  - _Ví dụ_: Viet Nam has a rich and diverse culture.
- **ancestor (n) /ˈænsestə/**: tổ tiên.
  - _Collocation_: worship one's ancestors.
  - _Ví dụ_: People light incense to remember their ancestors.
- **terrace (v) /ˈterəs/**: làm ruộng bậc thang, tạo bậc thang.
  - _Collocation_: terrace the hillside.
  - _Ví dụ_: The farmers terrace the hillside to grow rice.
