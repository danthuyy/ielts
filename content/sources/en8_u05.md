## Unit 5: Our Customs and Traditions

- **tradition (n) /trəˈdɪʃn/**: truyền thống.
  - _Collocation_: keep a tradition, a family tradition.
  - _Ví dụ_: Giving lucky money at Tet is a lovely tradition.
- **generation (n) /ˌdʒenəˈreɪʃn/**: thế hệ.
  - _Collocation_: from generation to generation.
  - _Ví dụ_: This story is passed from generation to generation.
- **worship (v) /ˈwɜːʃɪp/**: thờ cúng.
  - _Collocation_: worship one's ancestors.
  - _Ví dụ_: Vietnamese families worship their ancestors at Tet.
- **incense (n) /ˈɪnsens/**: hương, nhang.
  - _Collocation_: burn incense.
  - _Ví dụ_: My grandmother burns incense on the first day of the month.
- **altar (n) /ˈɔːltə/**: bàn thờ.
  - _Collocation_: the family altar.
  - _Ví dụ_: We put fruit and flowers on the family altar.
- **offering (n) /ˈɒfərɪŋ/**: đồ cúng, lễ vật.
  - _Collocation_: make an offering.
  - _Ví dụ_: People bring offerings of fruit to the temple.
- **superstition (n) /ˌsuːpəˈstɪʃn/**: sự mê tín.
  - _Collocation_: an old superstition.
  - _Ví dụ_: Not sweeping the floor at Tet is an old superstition.
- **taboo (n) /təˈbuː/**: điều cấm kỵ.
  - _Collocation_: a cultural taboo.
  - _Ví dụ_: Breaking a plate at Tet is a taboo in some families.
- **lucky money (n) /ˈlʌki ˈmʌni/**: tiền lì xì.
  - _Collocation_: give lucky money.
  - _Ví dụ_: Children receive lucky money in red envelopes at Tet.
- **table manners (n) /ˈteɪbl ˈmænəz/**: phép lịch sự trên bàn ăn.
  - _Collocation_: have good table manners.
  - _Ví dụ_: Good table manners are important in a Vietnamese meal.
- **respect (v) /rɪˈspekt/**: tôn trọng, kính trọng.
  - _Collocation_: respect the elderly.
  - _Ví dụ_: Vietnamese children are taught to respect the elderly.
- **elderly (adj) /ˈeldəli/**: cao tuổi, lớn tuổi.
  - _Collocation_: an elderly person.
  - _Ví dụ_: We should give our seat to an elderly person on the bus.
- **greet (v) /ɡriːt/**: chào hỏi.
  - _Collocation_: greet guests.
  - _Ví dụ_: We greet our grandparents first when we arrive.
- **decorate (v) /ˈdekəreɪt/**: trang trí.
  - _Collocation_: decorate the house.
  - _Ví dụ_: My family decorates the house with peach blossoms.
- **wrap (v) /ræp/**: gói.
  - _Collocation_: wrap banh chung, wrap a gift.
  - _Ví dụ_: We wrap banh chung together on the day before Tet.
- **gather (v) /ˈɡæðə/**: tụ họp, quây quần.
  - _Collocation_: gather around the table.
  - _Ví dụ_: The whole family gathers around the table on New Year's Eve.
- **firework (n) /ˈfaɪəwɜːk/**: pháo hoa.
  - _Collocation_: watch fireworks.
  - _Ví dụ_: We watch fireworks in the town square at midnight.
- **ceremony (n) /ˈserəməni/**: buổi lễ, nghi lễ.
  - _Collocation_: a wedding ceremony.
  - _Ví dụ_: The wedding ceremony took place at her family's home.
- **polite (adj) /pəˈlaɪt/**: lịch sự.
  - _Collocation_: be polite to someone.
  - _Ví dụ_: It is polite to use both hands when giving a gift.
- **pass down (phr) /ˌpɑːs ˈdaʊn/**: truyền lại (cho đời sau).
  - _Collocation_: pass down a custom.
  - _Ví dụ_: These customs are passed down to younger generations.
