## Unit 7: Environmental Protection

- **environment (n) /ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/**: môi trường.
  - _Collocation_: protect the environment.
  - _Ví dụ_: We should all do something to protect the environment.
- **protect (v) /prəˈtekt/**: bảo vệ.
  - _Collocation_: protect something from something.
  - _Ví dụ_: These trees protect the village from strong winds.
- **pollution (n) /pəˈluːʃn/**: sự ô nhiễm.
  - _Collocation_: air pollution, water pollution.
  - _Ví dụ_: Air pollution in big cities is getting worse every year.
- **pollute (v) /pəˈluːt/**: làm ô nhiễm.
  - _Collocation_: pollute the river.
  - _Ví dụ_: The factory polluted the river with dirty water.
- **recycle (v) /ˌriːˈsaɪkl/**: tái chế.
  - _Collocation_: recycle paper and plastic.
  - _Ví dụ_: My class collects old newspapers and recycles them.
- **reduce (v) /rɪˈdjuːs/**: giảm bớt.
  - _Collocation_: reduce waste.
  - _Ví dụ_: We can reduce waste by using cloth bags.
- **reuse (v) /ˌriːˈjuːz/**: dùng lại, tái sử dụng.
  - _Collocation_: reuse a bottle.
  - _Ví dụ_: I reuse this glass bottle to keep drinking water.
- **deforestation (n) /ˌdiːˌfɒrɪˈsteɪʃn/**: nạn phá rừng.
  - _Collocation_: cause deforestation.
  - _Ví dụ_: Deforestation makes many animals lose their homes.
- **litter (n) /ˈlɪtə(r)/**: rác vứt bừa bãi.
  - _Collocation_: drop litter.
  - _Ví dụ_: Please do not drop litter in the park.
- **rubbish (n) /ˈrʌbɪʃ/**: rác thải.
  - _Collocation_: throw away rubbish.
  - _Ví dụ_: We put the rubbish in the bin after the picnic.
- **waste (n) /weɪst/**: chất thải.
  - _Collocation_: household waste.
  - _Ví dụ_: The school teaches students how to sort waste.
- **endangered (adj) /ɪnˈdeɪndʒəd/**: có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng.
  - _Collocation_: endangered animals.
  - _Ví dụ_: Tigers are endangered animals in our country.
- **species (n) /ˈspiːʃiːz/**: loài (sinh vật).
  - _Collocation_: rare species.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many rare species live in this national park.
- **global warming (phr) /ˌɡləʊbl ˈwɔːmɪŋ/**: sự nóng lên toàn cầu.
  - _Collocation_: cause global warming.
  - _Ví dụ_: Burning coal and oil causes global warming.
- **greenhouse effect (phr) /ˈɡriːnhaʊs ɪˌfekt/**: hiệu ứng nhà kính.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our teacher explained the greenhouse effect with a simple picture.
- **natural resources (phr) /ˌnætʃrəl rɪˈzɔːsɪz/**: tài nguyên thiên nhiên.
  - _Collocation_: save natural resources.
  - _Ví dụ_: We must save natural resources for future generations.
- **harmful (adj) /ˈhɑːmfl/**: có hại.
  - _Collocation_: harmful to health.
  - _Ví dụ_: Smoke from motorbikes is harmful to our health.
- **damage (v) /ˈdæmɪdʒ/**: gây hại, phá hoại.
  - _Collocation_: damage the environment.
  - _Ví dụ_: Plastic bags damage the environment for hundreds of years.
- **raise awareness (phr) /ˌreɪz əˈweənəs/**: nâng cao nhận thức.
  - _Ví dụ_: The club made posters to raise awareness of clean water.
- **single-use plastic (phr) /ˌsɪŋɡl juːs ˈplæstɪk/**: nhựa dùng một lần.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our school has stopped using single-use plastic cups.
