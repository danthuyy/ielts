## Unit 11: Science and Technology

- **science (n) /ˈsaɪəns/**: khoa học.
  - _Collocation_: modern science.
  - _Ví dụ_: Modern science has changed the way we live.
- **scientist (n) /ˈsaɪəntɪst/**: nhà khoa học.
  - _Ví dụ_: The scientist spent ten years studying rice plants.
- **technology (n) /tekˈnɒlədʒi/**: công nghệ.
  - _Collocation_: modern technology.
  - _Ví dụ_: Modern technology helps farmers grow better crops.
- **invention (n) /ɪnˈvenʃn/**: phát minh.
  - _Collocation_: a useful invention.
  - _Ví dụ_: The light bulb is one of the most useful inventions ever.
- **invent (v) /ɪnˈvent/**: phát minh ra.
  - _Ví dụ_: My classmate invented a machine that waters plants.
- **discovery (n) /dɪˈskʌvəri/**: khám phá.
  - _Collocation_: make a discovery.
  - _Ví dụ_: The team made an important discovery about sea water.
- **experiment (n) /ɪkˈsperɪmənt/**: thí nghiệm.
  - _Collocation_: do an experiment.
  - _Ví dụ_: We did a simple experiment with salt and water in class.
- **laboratory (n) /ləˈbɒrətri/**: phòng thí nghiệm.
  - _Ví dụ_: Students must wear glasses in the laboratory.
- **robot (n) /ˈrəʊbɒt/**: người máy.
  - _Ví dụ_: This robot can clean the floor by itself.
- **artificial intelligence (phr) /ˌɑːtɪfɪʃl ɪnˈtelɪdʒəns/**: trí tuệ nhân tạo.
  - _Ví dụ_: Artificial intelligence can now check homework for teachers.
- **develop (v) /dɪˈveləp/**: phát triển.
  - _Collocation_: develop a new product.
  - _Ví dụ_: The company is developing a cheaper solar panel.
- **research (n) /rɪˈsɜːtʃ/**: nghiên cứu.
  - _Collocation_: do research on something.
  - _Ví dụ_: They are doing research on clean energy.
- **innovation (n) /ˌɪnəˈveɪʃn/**: sự đổi mới, cải tiến.
  - _Ví dụ_: This small innovation saves the factory a lot of water.
- **advance (n) /ədˈvɑːns/**: bước tiến, tiến bộ.
  - _Collocation_: advances in medicine.
  - _Ví dụ_: Advances in medicine have saved millions of lives.
- **automatic (adj) /ˌɔːtəˈmætɪk/**: tự động.
  - _Collocation_: an automatic door.
  - _Ví dụ_: The automatic door opens when you come near it.
- **engineer (n) /ˌendʒɪˈnɪə(r)/**: kỹ sư.
  - _Ví dụ_: My uncle is an engineer at a car factory.
- **solve (v) /sɒlv/**: giải quyết.
  - _Collocation_: solve a problem.
  - _Ví dụ_: Science can help us solve the problem of dirty water.
- **improve (v) /ɪmˈpruːv/**: cải thiện.
  - _Ví dụ_: New machines have improved life in the countryside.
- **digital (adj) /ˈdɪdʒɪtl/**: kỹ thuật số.
  - _Collocation_: a digital camera.
  - _Ví dụ_: We took photos of the experiment with a digital camera.
- **breakthrough (n) /ˈbreɪkθruː/**: bước đột phá.
  - _Ví dụ_: The new medicine was a real breakthrough for doctors.
- **benefit (n) /ˈbenɪfɪt/**: lợi ích.
  - _Collocation_: the benefits of technology.
  - _Ví dụ_: We talked about the benefits of technology in class.
