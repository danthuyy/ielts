## Unit 3: Teenagers

- **teenager (n) /ˈtiːneɪdʒə/**: thanh thiếu niên (13-19 tuổi).
  - _Collocation_: a Vietnamese teenager.
  - _Ví dụ_: Every teenager needs time to talk with their parents.
- **confident (adj) /ˈkɒnfɪdənt/**: tự tin.
  - _Collocation_: feel confident.
  - _Ví dụ_: Joining the club makes me feel more confident.
- **independent (adj) /ˌɪndɪˈpendənt/**: độc lập, tự lập.
  - _Collocation_: become independent.
  - _Ví dụ_: My parents want me to become more independent.
- **communicate (v) /kəˈmjuːnɪkeɪt/**: giao tiếp.
  - _Collocation_: communicate with others.
  - _Ví dụ_: Teenagers should communicate more with their family.
- **informal (adj) /ɪnˈfɔːml/**: thân mật, không trang trọng.
  - _Collocation_: informal language.
  - _Ví dụ_: We use informal language when we chat with friends.
- **manage (v) /ˈmænɪdʒ/**: quản lý, xoay xở.
  - _Collocation_: manage time, manage money.
  - _Ví dụ_: I am learning how to manage my time better.
- **housework (n) /ˈhaʊswɜːk/**: việc nhà.
  - _Collocation_: do the housework.
  - _Ví dụ_: I do the housework with my brother every evening.
- **life skill (n) /ˈlaɪf skɪl/**: kỹ năng sống.
  - _Collocation_: learn life skills.
  - _Ví dụ_: Cooking is a useful life skill for teenagers.
- **advice (n) /ədˈvaɪs/**: lời khuyên.
  - _Collocation_: give advice, ask for advice.
  - _Ví dụ_: My teacher gave me good advice about studying.
- **pressure (n) /ˈpreʃə/**: áp lực.
  - _Collocation_: study pressure, be under pressure.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many students are under pressure before the exams.
- **worried (adj) /ˈwʌrid/**: lo lắng.
  - _Collocation_: be worried about something.
  - _Ví dụ_: She is worried about her English test tomorrow.
- **share (v) /ʃeə/**: chia sẻ.
  - _Collocation_: share problems with someone.
  - _Ví dụ_: You should share your problems with your parents.
- **join (v) /dʒɔɪn/**: tham gia.
  - _Collocation_: join a club.
  - _Ví dụ_: I want to join the school football club this year.
- **club (n) /klʌb/**: câu lạc bộ.
  - _Collocation_: an English club.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our English club meets every Friday afternoon.
- **member (n) /ˈmembə/**: thành viên.
  - _Collocation_: a club member.
  - _Ví dụ_: There are twenty members in our music club.
- **choice (n) /tʃɔɪs/**: sự lựa chọn.
  - _Collocation_: make a choice.
  - _Ví dụ_: It is hard to make a choice between two hobbies.
- **future (n) /ˈfjuːtʃə/**: tương lai.
  - _Collocation_: in the future, plan for the future.
  - _Ví dụ_: I want to be a doctor in the future.
- **improve (v) /ɪmˈpruːv/**: cải thiện, nâng cao.
  - _Collocation_: improve one's English.
  - _Ví dụ_: Reading books helps me improve my English quickly.
- **helpline (n) /ˈhelplaɪn/**: đường dây trợ giúp.
  - _Collocation_: call a helpline.
  - _Ví dụ_: Teenagers can call a helpline when they need support.
- **hobby (n) /ˈhɒbi/**: sở thích.
  - _Collocation_: take up a hobby.
  - _Ví dụ_: My new hobby is growing small plants on the balcony.
