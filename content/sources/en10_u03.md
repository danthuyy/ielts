## Unit 3: Music

- **melody (n) /ˈmelədi/**: giai điệu.
  - _Collocation_: a beautiful melody.
  - _Ví dụ_: This song has a beautiful melody that stays in your head.
- **lyrics (n) /ˈlɪrɪks/**: lời bài hát.
  - _Collocation_: meaningful lyrics.
  - _Ví dụ_: I love this band because their lyrics are meaningful.
- **rhythm (n) /ˈrɪðəm/**: nhịp điệu.
  - _Collocation_: a fast rhythm.
  - _Ví dụ_: The song has a fast rhythm that makes people dance.
- **tune (n) /tjuːn/**: điệu nhạc, bản nhạc.
  - _Collocation_: catchy tune.
  - _Ví dụ_: She was humming a catchy tune all morning.
- **compose (v) /kəmˈpəʊz/**: sáng tác nhạc.
  - _Collocation_: compose a song.
  - _Ví dụ_: He composed his first song at the age of fifteen.
- **composer (n) /kəmˈpəʊzə/**: nhà soạn nhạc, nhạc sĩ.
  - _Collocation_: a famous composer.
  - _Ví dụ_: Van Cao is a famous Vietnamese composer.
- **perform (v) /pəˈfɔːm/**: biểu diễn.
  - _Collocation_: perform on stage.
  - _Ví dụ_: The band will perform on stage tonight.
- **audience (n) /ˈɔːdiəns/**: khán giả.
  - _Ví dụ_: The audience clapped loudly after the last song.
- **talent (n) /ˈtælənt/**: tài năng.
  - _Collocation_: musical talent.
  - _Ví dụ_: She showed her musical talent when she was very young.
- **contest (n) /ˈkɒntest/**: cuộc thi.
  - _Collocation_: a singing contest.
  - _Ví dụ_: He won first prize in a singing contest last year.
- **judge (n) /dʒʌdʒ/**: giám khảo.
  - _Ví dụ_: The judges gave her the highest score.
- **album (n) /ˈælbəm/**: an-bum, đĩa nhạc.
  - _Ví dụ_: Their new album became popular in just one week.
- **release (v) /rɪˈliːs/**: phát hành.
  - _Collocation_: release a single.
  - _Ví dụ_: The singer released a new single last month.
- **debut (n) /ˈdeɪbjuː/**: sự ra mắt lần đầu.
  - _Collocation_: make one's debut.
  - _Ví dụ_: The young singer made her debut on a TV show.
- **genre (n) /ˈʒɒnrə/**: thể loại (nhạc, phim).
  - _Ví dụ_: Pop is my favourite music genre.
- **folk music (n) /ˈfəʊk mjuːzɪk/**: nhạc dân gian.
  - _Ví dụ_: My grandmother often listens to Vietnamese folk music.
- **classical (adj) /ˈklæsɪkl/**: (nhạc) cổ điển.
  - _Collocation_: classical music.
  - _Ví dụ_: Classical music helps me relax while studying.
- **traditional (adj) /trəˈdɪʃənl/**: truyền thống.
  - _Collocation_: traditional instruments.
  - _Ví dụ_: The show featured traditional Vietnamese instruments.
- **instrument (n) /ˈɪnstrəmənt/**: nhạc cụ.
  - _Collocation_: play a musical instrument.
  - _Ví dụ_: Can you play any musical instrument?
- **band (n) /bænd/**: ban nhạc.
  - _Collocation_: form a band.
  - _Ví dụ_: My classmates and I formed a small band.
- **record (v) /rɪˈkɔːd/**: thu âm.
  - _Ví dụ_: They recorded the song in a studio in Ha Noi.
