## Unit 5: Inventions

- **invention (n) /ɪnˈvenʃn/**: phát minh.
  - _Collocation_: a great invention.
  - _Ví dụ_: The light bulb is one of the greatest inventions in history.
- **invent (v) /ɪnˈvent/**: phát minh ra.
  - _Ví dụ_: Who invented the telephone?
- **inventor (n) /ɪnˈventə/**: nhà phát minh.
  - _Ví dụ_: The young inventor won a national science prize.
- **device (n) /dɪˈvaɪs/**: thiết bị.
  - _Collocation_: an electronic device.
  - _Ví dụ_: This electronic device can measure your heart rate.
- **gadget (n) /ˈɡædʒɪt/**: đồ dùng công nghệ nhỏ gọn.
  - _Collocation_: a useful gadget.
  - _Ví dụ_: This kitchen gadget helps you peel fruit quickly.
- **technology (n) /tekˈnɒlədʒi/**: công nghệ.
  - _Collocation_: modern technology.
  - _Ví dụ_: Modern technology has changed the way we learn.
- **develop (v) /dɪˈveləp/**: phát triển, nghiên cứu chế tạo.
  - _Ví dụ_: Scientists are developing a cheaper solar panel.
- **design (v) /dɪˈzaɪn/**: thiết kế.
  - _Ví dụ_: She designed a robot that can clean the floor.
- **portable (adj) /ˈpɔːtəbl/**: xách tay, dễ mang theo.
  - _Collocation_: a portable charger.
  - _Ví dụ_: I always carry a portable charger in my bag.
- **wireless (adj) /ˈwaɪələs/**: không dây.
  - _Collocation_: wireless headphones.
  - _Ví dụ_: These wireless headphones last for ten hours.
- **convenient (adj) /kənˈviːniənt/**: tiện lợi.
  - _Ví dụ_: Online payment is convenient for busy people.
- **handy (adj) /ˈhændi/**: tiện dụng, hữu ích.
  - _Ví dụ_: This small torch is very handy when the power goes out.
- **function (n) /ˈfʌŋkʃn/**: chức năng.
  - _Ví dụ_: This watch has a function that counts your steps.
- **feature (n) /ˈfiːtʃə/**: tính năng, đặc điểm nổi bật.
  - _Collocation_: a useful feature.
  - _Ví dụ_: My favourite feature of the app is offline reading.
- **application (n) /ˌæplɪˈkeɪʃn/**: ứng dụng.
  - _Collocation_: a mobile application.
  - _Ví dụ_: I use a mobile application to learn new words every day.
- **software (n) /ˈsɒftweə/**: phần mềm.
  - _Ví dụ_: The school bought new software for the computer room.
- **drone (n) /drəʊn/**: máy bay không người lái.
  - _Ví dụ_: Farmers use drones to spray their rice fields.
- **robot (n) /ˈrəʊbɒt/**: rô-bốt.
  - _Ví dụ_: A robot can do dangerous work instead of people.
- **artificial intelligence (n) /ˌɑːtɪfɪʃl ɪnˈtelɪdʒəns/**: trí tuệ nhân tạo.
  - _Ví dụ_: Artificial intelligence can translate texts in seconds.
- **improve (v) /ɪmˈpruːv/**: cải thiện, làm tốt hơn.
  - _Ví dụ_: New inventions improve our daily lives.
- **patent (n) /ˈpætnt/**: bằng sáng chế.
  - _Ví dụ_: He got a patent for his new water filter.
- **breakthrough (n) /ˈbreɪkθruː/**: bước đột phá.
  - _Ví dụ_: The new battery is a real breakthrough in green energy.
