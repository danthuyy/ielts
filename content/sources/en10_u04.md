## Unit 4: For a Better Community

- **community (n) /kəˈmjuːnəti/**: cộng đồng.
  - _Collocation_: the local community.
  - _Ví dụ_: The club does many useful things for the local community.
- **volunteer (n) /ˌvɒlənˈtɪə/**: tình nguyện viên.
  - _Collocation_: work as a volunteer.
  - _Ví dụ_: She works as a volunteer at the children's hospital.
- **voluntary (adj) /ˈvɒləntri/**: tự nguyện, thiện nguyện.
  - _Collocation_: voluntary work.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many students do voluntary work in the summer.
- **charity (n) /ˈtʃærəti/**: tổ chức từ thiện; hoạt động từ thiện.
  - _Collocation_: a charity organisation.
  - _Ví dụ_: The money went to a charity for poor children.
- **donate (v) /dəʊˈneɪt/**: quyên góp, tặng.
  - _Collocation_: donate books to.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our class donated books to a rural school.
- **fundraising (n) /ˈfʌndreɪzɪŋ/**: việc gây quỹ.
  - _Collocation_: a fundraising event.
  - _Ví dụ_: The school held a fundraising event for flood victims.
- **raise funds (phr) /ˌreɪz ˈfʌndz/**: gây quỹ.
  - _Ví dụ_: We sold handmade cards to raise funds for the orphanage.
- **support (v) /səˈpɔːt/**: hỗ trợ, giúp đỡ.
  - _Ví dụ_: Local people supported the clean-up project.
- **disadvantaged (adj) /ˌdɪsədˈvɑːntɪdʒd/**: thiệt thòi, kém may mắn.
  - _Collocation_: disadvantaged children.
  - _Ví dụ_: The programme offers free classes to disadvantaged children.
- **orphanage (n) /ˈɔːfənɪdʒ/**: trại trẻ mồ côi.
  - _Ví dụ_: We visit the orphanage every Sunday afternoon.
- **the homeless (n) /ðə ˈhəʊmləs/**: những người vô gia cư.
  - _Ví dụ_: They give warm meals to the homeless in winter.
- **shelter (n) /ˈʃeltə/**: nơi trú ẩn, chỗ ở tạm.
  - _Ví dụ_: The charity built a shelter for people after the storm.
- **remote (adj) /rɪˈməʊt/**: xa xôi, hẻo lánh.
  - _Collocation_: a remote area.
  - _Ví dụ_: The teachers travel to a remote area every month.
- **rural (adj) /ˈrʊərəl/**: thuộc nông thôn.
  - _Collocation_: rural areas.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many children in rural areas need school supplies.
- **organise (v) /ˈɔːɡənaɪz/**: tổ chức.
  - _Collocation_: organise an activity.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our club organises a blood donation day every year.
- **campaign (n) /kæmˈpeɪn/**: chiến dịch.
  - _Collocation_: launch a campaign.
  - _Ví dụ_: They launched a campaign to plant trees in the city.
- **participate (v) /pɑːˈtɪsɪpeɪt/**: tham gia.
  - _Collocation_: participate in.
  - _Ví dụ_: More than 200 students participated in the event.
- **non-profit (adj) /ˌnɒn ˈprɒfɪt/**: phi lợi nhuận.
  - _Collocation_: a non-profit organisation.
  - _Ví dụ_: She works for a non-profit organisation in Hue.
- **generous (adj) /ˈdʒenərəs/**: hào phóng, rộng lượng.
  - _Ví dụ_: The villagers were very generous with their time and food.
- **meaningful (adj) /ˈmiːnɪŋfl/**: có ý nghĩa.
  - _Ví dụ_: Volunteering is a meaningful way to spend the summer.
- **tutor (v) /ˈtjuːtə/**: dạy kèm.
  - _Ví dụ_: He tutors poor children in maths after school.
