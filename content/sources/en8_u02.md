## Unit 2: Life in the Countryside

- **countryside (n) /ˈkʌntrisaɪd/**: vùng nông thôn, miền quê.
  - _Collocation_: live in the countryside.
  - _Ví dụ_: My grandparents live in the countryside near a river.
- **buffalo (n) /ˈbʌfələʊ/**: con trâu.
  - _Collocation_: ride a buffalo, herd buffaloes.
  - _Ví dụ_: The boy rides a buffalo home every afternoon.
- **harvest (v) /ˈhɑːvɪst/**: thu hoạch.
  - _Collocation_: harvest the rice.
  - _Ví dụ_: Farmers harvest the rice in early October.
- **paddy field (n) /ˈpædi fiːld/**: cánh đồng lúa.
  - _Collocation_: work in the paddy field.
  - _Ví dụ_: The green paddy fields look beautiful in the morning.
- **crop (n) /krɒp/**: vụ mùa, cây trồng.
  - _Collocation_: grow crops.
  - _Ví dụ_: My uncle grows crops such as maize and beans.
- **cattle (n) /ˈkætl/**: gia súc (trâu, bò).
  - _Collocation_: herd the cattle.
  - _Ví dụ_: The children help their parents herd the cattle.
- **orchard (n) /ˈɔːtʃəd/**: vườn cây ăn quả.
  - _Collocation_: a fruit orchard.
  - _Ví dụ_: We picked oranges in my grandfather's orchard.
- **peaceful (adj) /ˈpiːsfl/**: yên bình, thanh bình.
  - _Collocation_: a peaceful village.
  - _Ví dụ_: Life in this peaceful village is slow and quiet.
- **inconvenient (adj) /ˌɪnkənˈviːniənt/**: bất tiện.
  - _Collocation_: an inconvenient journey.
  - _Ví dụ_: Travelling to school is inconvenient without a bus.
- **vast (adj) /vɑːst/**: rộng lớn, bao la.
  - _Collocation_: a vast field.
  - _Ví dụ_: From the hill, we can see a vast green field.
- **nomad (n) /ˈnəʊmæd/**: người du mục.
  - _Collocation_: a nomad family.
  - _Ví dụ_: A nomad moves from place to place to find grass for animals.
- **herd (v) /hɜːd/**: chăn (gia súc), lùa.
  - _Collocation_: herd sheep.
  - _Ví dụ_: He herds the sheep to the mountain every morning.
- **harvest time (n) /ˈhɑːvɪst taɪm/**: mùa gặt, mùa thu hoạch.
  - _Collocation_: at harvest time.
  - _Ví dụ_: The whole village is busy at harvest time.
- **collect (v) /kəˈlekt/**: thu gom, nhặt.
  - _Collocation_: collect water, collect eggs.
  - _Ví dụ_: The girls collect eggs from the hen house every day.
- **dry (v) /draɪ/**: phơi khô, làm khô.
  - _Collocation_: dry the rice.
  - _Ví dụ_: The farmers dry the rice in the sun after harvesting.
- **pick (v) /pɪk/**: hái, nhặt.
  - _Collocation_: pick fruit, pick flowers.
  - _Ví dụ_: We picked some fresh fruit in the garden.
- **kite (n) /kaɪt/**: cái diều.
  - _Collocation_: fly a kite.
  - _Ví dụ_: The village children fly kites on windy afternoons.
- **fresh (adj) /freʃ/**: trong lành, tươi.
  - _Collocation_: fresh air, fresh vegetables.
  - _Ví dụ_: I love the fresh air in the countryside.
- **generous (adj) /ˈdʒenərəs/**: hào phóng, rộng lượng.
  - _Collocation_: generous people.
  - _Ví dụ_: The villagers are generous and always help each other.
- **slow (adj) /sləʊ/**: chậm rãi.
  - _Collocation_: a slow pace of life.
  - _Ví dụ_: I like the slow pace of life in my grandmother's village.
