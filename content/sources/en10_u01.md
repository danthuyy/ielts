## Unit 1: Family Life

- **household chores (n) /ˌhaʊshəʊld ˈtʃɔːz/**: việc nhà.
  - _Collocation_: do the household chores.
  - _Ví dụ_: My brother and I share the household chores every weekend.
- **laundry (n) /ˈlɔːndri/**: việc giặt giũ; quần áo cần giặt.
  - _Collocation_: do the laundry.
  - _Ví dụ_: My father does the laundry twice a week.
- **washing-up (n) /ˌwɒʃɪŋ ˈʌp/**: việc rửa bát đĩa.
  - _Collocation_: do the washing-up.
  - _Ví dụ_: I always do the washing-up after dinner.
- **breadwinner (n) /ˈbredwɪnə/**: trụ cột kinh tế của gia đình.
  - _Collocation_: the main breadwinner.
  - _Ví dụ_: My mother is the main breadwinner in our family.
- **homemaker (n) /ˈhəʊmmeɪkə/**: người nội trợ, người lo việc nhà.
  - _Ví dụ_: Being a homemaker is hard work that is often forgotten.
- **responsibility (n) /rɪˌspɒnsəˈbɪləti/**: trách nhiệm.
  - _Collocation_: take responsibility for.
  - _Ví dụ_: Children should take responsibility for their own rooms.
- **grocery (n) /ˈɡrəʊsəri/**: thực phẩm, hàng tạp hoá.
  - _Collocation_: do the grocery shopping.
  - _Ví dụ_: My parents do the grocery shopping on Sunday morning.
- **rubbish (n) /ˈrʌbɪʃ/**: rác.
  - _Collocation_: take out the rubbish.
  - _Ví dụ_: It is my job to take out the rubbish every evening.
- **heavy lifting (n) /ˌhevi ˈlɪftɪŋ/**: việc nặng, việc phải khiêng vác.
  - _Ví dụ_: My dad does the heavy lifting when we move furniture.
- **iron (v) /ˈaɪən/**: là, ủi (quần áo).
  - _Collocation_: iron the clothes.
  - _Ví dụ_: My sister irons her uniform before school.
- **mop (v) /mɒp/**: lau (nhà) bằng cây lau.
  - _Collocation_: mop the floor.
  - _Ví dụ_: I mop the floor twice a week.
- **vacuum (v) /ˈvækjuːm/**: hút bụi.
  - _Collocation_: vacuum the carpet.
  - _Ví dụ_: She vacuums the living room every Saturday.
- **tidy up (phr) /ˌtaɪdi ˈʌp/**: dọn dẹp cho gọn gàng.
  - _Ví dụ_: Please tidy up your desk before you go to bed.
- **split (v) /splɪt/**: chia (công việc, chi phí).
  - _Collocation_: split the housework.
  - _Ví dụ_: My parents split the housework equally.
- **routine (n) /ruːˈtiːn/**: thói quen, nếp sinh hoạt hằng ngày.
  - _Collocation_: daily routine.
  - _Ví dụ_: Helping my mum cook is part of my daily routine.
- **bond (n) /bɒnd/**: sự gắn bó, mối gắn kết.
  - _Collocation_: a strong family bond.
  - _Ví dụ_: Eating together creates a strong bond between family members.
- **strengthen (v) /ˈstreŋθn/**: làm cho bền chặt hơn.
  - _Collocation_: strengthen relationships.
  - _Ví dụ_: Sharing chores strengthens the relationship between parents and children.
- **benefit (n) /ˈbenɪfɪt/**: lợi ích.
  - _Ví dụ_: Doing chores has many benefits for teenagers.
- **contribute (v) /kənˈtrɪbjuːt/**: đóng góp.
  - _Collocation_: contribute to the family.
  - _Ví dụ_: Every member should contribute to the family in some way.
- **financial (adj) /faɪˈnænʃl/**: thuộc về tài chính, tiền bạc.
  - _Collocation_: financial support.
  - _Ví dụ_: My uncle gives financial support to his old parents.
