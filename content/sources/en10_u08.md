## Unit 8: New Ways to Learn

- **device (n) /dɪˈvaɪs/**: thiết bị.
  - _Collocation_: an electronic device, a mobile device.
  - _Ví dụ_: Students use electronic devices to look up new words in class.
- **application (n) /ˌæplɪˈkeɪʃn/**: ứng dụng (phần mềm).
  - _Collocation_: a learning application, install an application.
  - _Ví dụ_: This application helps me practise listening every evening.
- **download (v) /ˌdaʊnˈləʊd/**: tải xuống.
  - _Collocation_: download a file, download an app.
  - _Ví dụ_: You can download the lesson and study it offline.
- **access (v) /ˈækses/**: truy cập.
  - _Collocation_: access the Internet, access information.
  - _Ví dụ_: We can access thousands of free lessons on the Internet.
- **upload (v) /ˌʌpˈləʊd/**: tải lên.
  - _Collocation_: upload a video, upload homework.
  - _Ví dụ_: Please upload your homework to the class website before Friday.
- **online (adj) /ˌɒnˈlaɪn/**: trực tuyến.
  - _Collocation_: an online course, online learning.
  - _Ví dụ_: She is taking an online course in graphic design.
- **benefit (n) /ˈbenɪfɪt/**: lợi ích.
  - _Collocation_: the benefits of something, bring benefits.
  - _Ví dụ_: One benefit of digital learning is that you can study anywhere.
- **distraction (n) /dɪˈstrækʃn/**: sự xao nhãng, điều gây mất tập trung.
  - _Collocation_: a major distraction, avoid distractions.
  - _Ví dụ_: Social media can be a major distraction while you are studying.
- **effective (adj) /ɪˈfektɪv/**: hiệu quả.
  - _Collocation_: an effective method, highly effective.
  - _Ví dụ_: Watching films with subtitles is an effective way to learn English.
- **concentrate (v) /ˈkɒnsntreɪt/**: tập trung.
  - _Collocation_: concentrate on something.
  - _Ví dụ_: I turn off my phone so that I can concentrate on my homework.
- **interactive (adj) /ˌɪntərˈæktɪv/**: có tính tương tác.
  - _Collocation_: an interactive lesson, an interactive whiteboard.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our teacher uses an interactive whiteboard in every lesson.
- **portable (adj) /ˈpɔːtəbl/**: dễ mang theo, xách tay.
  - _Collocation_: a portable device, small and portable.
  - _Ví dụ_: A tablet is portable, so I can study on the bus.
- **digital (adj) /ˈdɪdʒɪtl/**: kỹ thuật số.
  - _Collocation_: a digital dictionary, digital skills.
  - _Ví dụ_: I use a digital dictionary to check pronunciation.
- **research (v) /rɪˈsɜːtʃ/**: nghiên cứu, tra cứu.
  - _Collocation_: research a topic, research information.
  - _Ví dụ_: We researched the topic online before writing the report.
- **assignment (n) /əˈsaɪnmənt/**: bài tập được giao.
  - _Collocation_: hand in an assignment, complete an assignment.
  - _Ví dụ_: I have to hand in my history assignment tomorrow.
- **convenient (adj) /kənˈviːniənt/**: tiện lợi.
  - _Collocation_: convenient for someone, quick and convenient.
  - _Ví dụ_: Learning with an app is convenient for busy students.
- **flexible (adj) /ˈfleksəbl/**: linh hoạt.
  - _Collocation_: a flexible timetable, flexible learning.
  - _Ví dụ_: Online classes give me a flexible timetable.
- **virtual (adj) /ˈvɜːtʃuəl/**: ảo.
  - _Collocation_: a virtual classroom, virtual reality.
  - _Ví dụ_: In a virtual classroom, students can talk to teachers from home.
- **eyestrain (n) /ˈaɪstreɪn/**: chứng mỏi mắt.
  - _Collocation_: cause eyestrain, suffer from eyestrain.
  - _Ví dụ_: Looking at a screen for hours can cause eyestrain.
- **efficiently (adv) /ɪˈfɪʃntli/**: một cách hiệu quả.
  - _Collocation_: work efficiently, learn efficiently.
  - _Ví dụ_: Good apps help students learn more efficiently.
- **take notes (phr) /ˌteɪk ˈnəʊts/**: ghi chép.
  - _Collocation_: take notes in class.
  - _Ví dụ_: I take notes on my laptop during every lesson.
- **screen (n) /skriːn/**: màn hình.
  - _Collocation_: screen time, look at a screen.
  - _Ví dụ_: Doctors advise teenagers to limit their screen time.
