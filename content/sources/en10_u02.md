## Unit 2: Humans and the Environment

- **environment (n) /ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/**: môi trường.
  - _Collocation_: protect the environment.
  - _Ví dụ_: We must protect the environment for future generations.
- **pollution (n) /pəˈluːʃn/**: sự ô nhiễm.
  - _Collocation_: air pollution.
  - _Ví dụ_: Air pollution in big cities is getting worse.
- **deforestation (n) /ˌdiːˌfɒrɪˈsteɪʃn/**: nạn phá rừng.
  - _Ví dụ_: Deforestation destroys the homes of many wild animals.
- **greenhouse gas (n) /ˈɡriːnhaʊs ɡæs/**: khí nhà kính.
  - _Ví dụ_: Cars and factories produce a lot of greenhouse gases.
- **climate change (n) /ˈklaɪmət tʃeɪndʒ/**: biến đổi khí hậu.
  - _Collocation_: fight climate change.
  - _Ví dụ_: Climate change causes stronger storms and longer droughts.
- **carbon footprint (n) /ˈkɑːbən ˈfʊtprɪnt/**: dấu chân các-bon (lượng khí thải gây ra).
  - _Collocation_: reduce your carbon footprint.
  - _Ví dụ_: Cycling to school helps reduce your carbon footprint.
- **emission (n) /ɪˈmɪʃn/**: khí thải, sự thải ra.
  - _Collocation_: cut emissions.
  - _Ví dụ_: The government wants to cut emissions from factories.
- **recycle (v) /ˌriːˈsaɪkl/**: tái chế.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our school recycles paper and plastic bottles.
- **reuse (v) /ˌriːˈjuːz/**: tái sử dụng.
  - _Ví dụ_: You can reuse this bag many times.
- **reduce (v) /rɪˈdjuːs/**: giảm, làm giảm bớt.
  - _Collocation_: reduce waste.
  - _Ví dụ_: We should reduce the amount of water we use.
- **waste (n) /weɪst/**: rác thải, chất thải.
  - _Collocation_: plastic waste.
  - _Ví dụ_: Plastic waste is harming sea animals.
- **packaging (n) /ˈpækɪdʒɪŋ/**: bao bì đóng gói.
  - _Ví dụ_: Try to buy products with less packaging.
- **sustainable (adj) /səˈsteɪnəbl/**: bền vững.
  - _Collocation_: a sustainable lifestyle.
  - _Ví dụ_: A sustainable lifestyle uses fewer resources.
- **eco-friendly (adj) /ˌiːkəʊ ˈfrendli/**: thân thiện với môi trường.
  - _Collocation_: eco-friendly products.
  - _Ví dụ_: This shop only sells eco-friendly products.
- **energy (n) /ˈenədʒi/**: năng lượng.
  - _Collocation_: save energy.
  - _Ví dụ_: Turn off the lights to save energy.
- **conserve (v) /kənˈsɜːv/**: bảo tồn, tiết kiệm.
  - _Collocation_: conserve water.
  - _Ví dụ_: We should conserve water during the dry season.
- **endangered (adj) /ɪnˈdeɪndʒəd/**: có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng.
  - _Collocation_: an endangered species.
  - _Ví dụ_: The Javan rhino is an endangered animal in Viet Nam.
- **species (n) /ˈspiːʃiːz/**: loài (sinh vật).
  - _Ví dụ_: Thousands of species disappear every year.
- **habitat (n) /ˈhæbɪtæt/**: môi trường sống của động thực vật.
  - _Collocation_: natural habitat.
  - _Ví dụ_: Forest fires destroy the natural habitat of many birds.
- **natural resources (n) /ˌnætʃrəl rɪˈzɔːsɪz/**: tài nguyên thiên nhiên.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our country is rich in natural resources.
- **litter (v) /ˈlɪtə/**: xả rác bừa bãi.
  - _Ví dụ_: Please do not litter in the park.
- **awareness (n) /əˈweənəs/**: nhận thức, sự hiểu biết.
  - _Collocation_: raise awareness of.
  - _Ví dụ_: The club raises awareness of environmental problems.
