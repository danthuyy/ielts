## Unit 9: Protecting the Environment

- **environment (n) /ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/**: môi trường.
  - _Collocation_: protect the environment, harm the environment.
  - _Ví dụ_: We should all do something to protect the environment.
- **pollution (n) /pəˈluːʃn/**: sự ô nhiễm.
  - _Collocation_: air pollution, water pollution.
  - _Ví dụ_: Air pollution in big cities is getting worse every year.
- **pollutant (n) /pəˈluːtənt/**: chất gây ô nhiễm.
  - _Collocation_: release pollutants, harmful pollutants.
  - _Ví dụ_: Factories release pollutants into the river.
- **deforestation (n) /ˌdiːˌfɒrɪˈsteɪʃn/**: nạn phá rừng.
  - _Collocation_: cause deforestation, stop deforestation.
  - _Ví dụ_: Deforestation destroys the homes of many wild animals.
- **contaminate (v) /kənˈtæmɪneɪt/**: làm nhiễm bẩn, làm ô nhiễm.
  - _Collocation_: contaminate water, contaminate the soil.
  - _Ví dụ_: Chemicals from the factory have contaminated the water supply.
- **greenhouse gas (phr) /ˈɡriːnhaʊs ɡæs/**: khí nhà kính.
  - _Collocation_: greenhouse gas emissions.
  - _Ví dụ_: Cars and factories produce a lot of greenhouse gases.
- **climate change (phr) /ˈklaɪmət tʃeɪndʒ/**: biến đổi khí hậu.
  - _Collocation_: fight climate change, the effects of climate change.
  - _Ví dụ_: Climate change is causing longer and hotter summers.
- **emission (n) /ɪˈmɪʃn/**: khí thải, sự phát thải.
  - _Collocation_: carbon emissions, cut emissions.
  - _Ví dụ_: The government wants to cut carbon emissions by half.
- **waste (n) /weɪst/**: chất thải, rác thải.
  - _Collocation_: industrial waste, dump waste.
  - _Ví dụ_: The factory dumped its waste into the sea.
- **recycle (v) /ˌriːˈsaɪkl/**: tái chế.
  - _Collocation_: recycle paper, recycle plastic bottles.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our school recycles paper and plastic bottles every week.
- **reduce (v) /rɪˈdjuːs/**: giảm bớt.
  - _Collocation_: reduce waste, reduce pollution.
  - _Ví dụ_: Using public transport can reduce air pollution.
- **destroy (v) /dɪˈstrɔɪ/**: phá huỷ.
  - _Collocation_: destroy forests, destroy habitats.
  - _Ví dụ_: Forest fires destroy thousands of hectares every year.
- **endangered (adj) /ɪnˈdeɪndʒəd/**: có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng.
  - _Collocation_: an endangered species, endangered animals.
  - _Ví dụ_: The Sao la is an endangered animal found in Viet Nam.
- **species (n) /ˈspiːʃiːz/**: loài.
  - _Collocation_: a rare species, protect species.
  - _Ví dụ_: Hundreds of species disappear from the earth every year.
- **habitat (n) /ˈhæbɪtæt/**: môi trường sống (của sinh vật).
  - _Collocation_: a natural habitat, lose their habitat.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many birds have lost their natural habitat because of farming.
- **ecosystem (n) /ˈiːkəʊsɪstəm/**: hệ sinh thái.
  - _Collocation_: a fragile ecosystem, damage an ecosystem.
  - _Ví dụ_: Plastic waste is damaging the ocean ecosystem.
- **awareness (n) /əˈweənəs/**: nhận thức, sự hiểu biết.
  - _Collocation_: raise awareness of something.
  - _Ví dụ_: The club organises talks to raise awareness of recycling.
- **protect (v) /prəˈtekt/**: bảo vệ.
  - _Collocation_: protect wild animals, protect nature.
  - _Ví dụ_: Laws have been passed to protect wild animals from hunters.
- **natural resources (phr) /ˌnætʃrəl rɪˈzɔːsɪz/**: tài nguyên thiên nhiên.
  - _Collocation_: use natural resources, save natural resources.
  - _Ví dụ_: We must use our natural resources wisely.
- **harmful (adj) /ˈhɑːmfl/**: có hại.
  - _Collocation_: harmful to health, harmful chemicals.
  - _Ví dụ_: Burning rubbish releases gases that are harmful to health.
- **litter (n) /ˈlɪtə/**: rác vứt bừa bãi.
  - _Collocation_: drop litter, pick up litter.
  - _Ví dụ_: Please do not drop litter in the park.
- **soil (n) /sɔɪl/**: đất trồng.
  - _Collocation_: soil pollution, rich soil.
  - _Ví dụ_: Chemical fertilisers can damage the soil over time.
