## Unit 1: Leisure Time

- **leisure (n) /ˈleʒə/**: thời gian rảnh, sự giải trí.
  - _Collocation_: leisure time, leisure activities.
  - _Ví dụ_: In my leisure time, I often read comic books.
- **hang out (phr) /ˌhæŋ ˈaʊt/**: đi chơi, la cà (với bạn bè).
  - _Collocation_: hang out with friends.
  - _Ví dụ_: We usually hang out at the park after school.
- **socialise (v) /ˈsəʊʃəlaɪz/**: giao lưu, kết bạn.
  - _Collocation_: socialise with classmates.
  - _Ví dụ_: My sister likes to socialise with people from other countries.
- **craft (n) /krɑːft/**: nghề thủ công, đồ thủ công.
  - _Collocation_: make crafts, craft kit.
  - _Ví dụ_: She makes beautiful crafts from old paper.
- **origami (n) /ˌɒrɪˈɡɑːmi/**: nghệ thuật gấp giấy Nhật Bản.
  - _Collocation_: do origami.
  - _Ví dụ_: My brother can do origami and make a paper crane.
- **knit (v) /nɪt/**: đan (len).
  - _Collocation_: knit a scarf.
  - _Ví dụ_: My grandmother taught me how to knit a warm scarf.
- **DIY (n) /ˌdiː aɪ ˈwaɪ/**: tự làm đồ (do it yourself).
  - _Collocation_: a DIY project.
  - _Ví dụ_: I found a fun DIY project to decorate my bedroom.
- **pastime (n) /ˈpɑːstaɪm/**: thú tiêu khiển.
  - _Collocation_: a favourite pastime.
  - _Ví dụ_: Playing badminton is my favourite pastime.
- **relaxing (adj) /rɪˈlæksɪŋ/**: thư giãn, làm cho thoải mái.
  - _Collocation_: a relaxing activity.
  - _Ví dụ_: Listening to soft music is very relaxing after a long day.
- **addicted (adj) /əˈdɪktɪd/**: nghiện, mê mẩn.
  - _Collocation_: be addicted to games.
  - _Ví dụ_: Some students are addicted to online games.
- **community (n) /kəˈmjuːnəti/**: cộng đồng.
  - _Collocation_: community activities.
  - _Ví dụ_: My class joins community activities every summer.
- **volunteer (v) /ˌvɒlənˈtɪə/**: làm tình nguyện.
  - _Collocation_: volunteer at a centre.
  - _Ví dụ_: We volunteer at an old people's centre on Sundays.
- **board game (n) /ˈbɔːd ɡeɪm/**: trò chơi trên bàn cờ.
  - _Collocation_: play board games.
  - _Ví dụ_: My family plays board games together on rainy evenings.
- **puzzle (n) /ˈpʌzl/**: trò chơi ghép hình, câu đố.
  - _Collocation_: do a puzzle, solve a puzzle.
  - _Ví dụ_: It took me two hours to do this puzzle.
- **netizen (n) /ˈnetɪzn/**: cư dân mạng.
  - _Collocation_: young netizens.
  - _Ví dụ_: Many young netizens share their videos every day.
- **satisfied (adj) /ˈsætɪsfaɪd/**: hài lòng, thoả mãn.
  - _Collocation_: be satisfied with something.
  - _Ví dụ_: I am satisfied with my new hobby.
- **spend (v) /spend/**: dành (thời gian), tiêu (tiền).
  - _Collocation_: spend time doing something.
  - _Ví dụ_: He spends two hours a day playing the guitar.
- **enjoy (v) /ɪnˈdʒɔɪ/**: thích, tận hưởng.
  - _Collocation_: enjoy doing something.
  - _Ví dụ_: I enjoy watching cooking shows at the weekend.
- **avoid (v) /əˈvɔɪd/**: tránh, né.
  - _Collocation_: avoid doing something.
  - _Ví dụ_: You should avoid using your phone before bedtime.
- **stress (n) /stres/**: sự căng thẳng.
  - _Collocation_: reduce stress.
  - _Ví dụ_: Doing exercise helps me reduce stress after exams.
