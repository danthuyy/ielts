## Unit 10: Ecotourism

- **ecotourism (n) /ˈiːkəʊtʊərɪzəm/**: du lịch sinh thái.
  - _Collocation_: develop ecotourism, an ecotourism site.
  - _Ví dụ_: Ecotourism helps people enjoy nature without damaging it.
- **tourist (n) /ˈtʊərɪst/**: khách du lịch.
  - _Collocation_: attract tourists, foreign tourists.
  - _Ví dụ_: Sa Pa attracts thousands of foreign tourists each year.
- **destination (n) /ˌdestɪˈneɪʃn/**: điểm đến.
  - _Collocation_: a popular destination, a holiday destination.
  - _Ví dụ_: Phong Nha has become a popular destination for young travellers.
- **attraction (n) /əˈtrækʃn/**: điểm hấp dẫn, thắng cảnh.
  - _Collocation_: a tourist attraction, a natural attraction.
  - _Ví dụ_: Ha Long Bay is the most famous tourist attraction in the north.
- **accommodation (n) /əˌkɒməˈdeɪʃn/**: chỗ ở.
  - _Collocation_: book accommodation, cheap accommodation.
  - _Ví dụ_: We booked accommodation at a small homestay in the village.
- **local (adj) /ˈləʊkl/**: thuộc địa phương.
  - _Collocation_: local people, local food.
  - _Ví dụ_: Eating local food is one of the best parts of the trip.
- **guide (n) /ɡaɪd/**: hướng dẫn viên.
  - _Collocation_: a tour guide, hire a guide.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our guide told us the story of the cave.
- **explore (v) /ɪkˈsplɔː/**: khám phá.
  - _Collocation_: explore a cave, explore the countryside.
  - _Ví dụ_: We spent two days exploring the mangrove forest by boat.
- **landscape (n) /ˈlændskeɪp/**: phong cảnh.
  - _Collocation_: a beautiful landscape, a mountain landscape.
  - _Ví dụ_: The landscape around the lake is breathtaking at sunrise.
- **wildlife (n) /ˈwaɪldlaɪf/**: động vật hoang dã.
  - _Collocation_: wildlife conservation, watch wildlife.
  - _Ví dụ_: Cuc Phuong National Park is famous for its rich wildlife.
- **preserve (v) /prɪˈzɜːv/**: bảo tồn, gìn giữ.
  - _Collocation_: preserve nature, preserve traditions.
  - _Ví dụ_: Ecotourism can help local people preserve their traditions.
- **biodiversity (n) /ˌbaɪəʊdaɪˈvɜːsəti/**: đa dạng sinh học.
  - _Collocation_: rich biodiversity, protect biodiversity.
  - _Ví dụ_: The park is well known for its rich biodiversity.
- **impact (n) /ˈɪmpækt/**: tác động, ảnh hưởng.
  - _Collocation_: a negative impact, have an impact on.
  - _Ví dụ_: Mass tourism has a negative impact on small islands.
- **heritage (n) /ˈherɪtɪdʒ/**: di sản.
  - _Collocation_: a World Heritage Site, cultural heritage.
  - _Ví dụ_: Hoi An Ancient Town is a World Heritage Site.
- **cultural (adj) /ˈkʌltʃərəl/**: thuộc văn hoá.
  - _Collocation_: cultural values, a cultural experience.
  - _Ví dụ_: Staying with a local family was a wonderful cultural experience.
- **souvenir (n) /ˌsuːvəˈnɪə/**: quà lưu niệm.
  - _Collocation_: buy a souvenir, a souvenir shop.
  - _Ví dụ_: I bought a handmade scarf as a souvenir from the trip.
- **trek (v) /trek/**: đi bộ đường dài (leo núi, băng rừng).
  - _Collocation_: trek through the jungle, go trekking.
  - _Ví dụ_: We trekked through the jungle for three hours to reach the waterfall.
- **national park (phr) /ˌnæʃnəl ˈpɑːk/**: vườn quốc gia.
  - _Collocation_: visit a national park.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our class visited Cat Tien National Park last summer.
- **experience (n) /ɪkˈspɪəriəns/**: trải nghiệm.
  - _Collocation_: an unforgettable experience, gain experience.
  - _Ví dụ_: Camping in the forest was an unforgettable experience.
- **eco-friendly (adj) /ˌiːkəʊ ˈfrendli/**: thân thiện với môi trường.
  - _Collocation_: eco-friendly products, an eco-friendly hotel.
  - _Ví dụ_: The resort uses eco-friendly bamboo straws instead of plastic ones.
- **unspoilt (adj) /ˌʌnˈspɔɪlt/**: hoang sơ, chưa bị tàn phá.
  - _Collocation_: an unspoilt beach, unspoilt scenery.
  - _Ví dụ_: They spent a week on a quiet, unspoilt beach in Phu Quoc.
- **income (n) /ˈɪnkʌm/**: thu nhập.
  - _Collocation_: a source of income, earn an income.
  - _Ví dụ_: Ecotourism provides a steady income for many villagers.
