## Unit 6: Lifestyles

- **lifestyle (n) /ˈlaɪfstaɪl/**: lối sống, phong cách sống.
  - _Collocation_: a healthy lifestyle.
  - _Ví dụ_: A healthy lifestyle includes exercise and good food.
- **urban (adj) /ˈɜːbən/**: thuộc thành thị.
  - _Collocation_: urban life, urban areas.
  - _Ví dụ_: Urban life is busy but full of opportunities.
- **rural (adj) /ˈrʊərəl/**: thuộc nông thôn.
  - _Collocation_: rural areas.
  - _Ví dụ_: People in rural areas often know all their neighbours.
- **convenient (adj) /kənˈviːniənt/**: tiện lợi.
  - _Collocation_: convenient transport.
  - _Ví dụ_: Living near the station is very convenient for me.
- **facility (n) /fəˈsɪləti/**: cơ sở vật chất, tiện ích.
  - _Collocation_: modern facilities.
  - _Ví dụ_: The new school has modern facilities for students.
- **eco-friendly (adj) /ˌiːkəʊ ˈfrendli/**: thân thiện với môi trường.
  - _Collocation_: an eco-friendly product.
  - _Ví dụ_: We use eco-friendly bags instead of plastic ones.
- **recycle (v) /ˌriːˈsaɪkl/**: tái chế.
  - _Collocation_: recycle paper and bottles.
  - _Ví dụ_: Our class recycles paper and plastic bottles every week.
- **reduce (v) /rɪˈdjuːs/**: giảm bớt.
  - _Collocation_: reduce waste.
  - _Ví dụ_: We should reduce waste at home and at school.
- **waste (n) /weɪst/**: rác thải, sự lãng phí.
  - _Collocation_: plastic waste.
  - _Ví dụ_: Plastic waste is a big problem in many cities.
- **energy (n) /ˈenədʒi/**: năng lượng.
  - _Collocation_: save energy.
  - _Ví dụ_: Turn off the lights to save energy.
- **habit (n) /ˈhæbɪt/**: thói quen.
  - _Collocation_: a good habit, a bad habit.
  - _Ví dụ_: Going to bed early is a good habit.
- **routine (n) /ruːˈtiːn/**: thói quen hằng ngày, lịch sinh hoạt.
  - _Collocation_: a daily routine.
  - _Ví dụ_: My daily routine starts with a short morning run.
- **balanced (adj) /ˈbælənst/**: cân bằng, cân đối.
  - _Collocation_: a balanced diet.
  - _Ví dụ_: A balanced diet keeps you strong and healthy.
- **modern (adj) /ˈmɒdn/**: hiện đại.
  - _Collocation_: a modern city.
  - _Ví dụ_: Modern technology changes the way we study.
- **technology (n) /tekˈnɒlədʒi/**: công nghệ.
  - _Collocation_: use technology.
  - _Ví dụ_: Students use technology to learn English at home.
- **pollution (n) /pəˈluːʃn/**: sự ô nhiễm.
  - _Collocation_: air pollution.
  - _Ví dụ_: Air pollution in big cities is getting worse.
- **traffic jam (n) /ˈtræfɪk dʒæm/**: tắc đường, kẹt xe.
  - _Collocation_: be stuck in a traffic jam.
  - _Ví dụ_: I was stuck in a traffic jam for thirty minutes.
- **crowded (adj) /ˈkraʊdɪd/**: đông đúc.
  - _Collocation_: a crowded street.
  - _Ví dụ_: The streets are crowded during rush hour.
- **cut down on (phr) /ˌkʌt ˈdaʊn ɒn/**: cắt giảm.
  - _Collocation_: cut down on fast food.
  - _Ví dụ_: You should cut down on fast food and sugary drinks.
- **cost of living (n) /ˌkɒst əv ˈlɪvɪŋ/**: chi phí sinh hoạt.
  - _Collocation_: a high cost of living.
  - _Ví dụ_: The cost of living in big cities is quite high.
