## Unit 8: Shopping

- **shopping centre (n) /ˈʃɒpɪŋ ˌsentə(r)/**: trung tâm mua sắm.
  - _Collocation_: go to a shopping centre.
  - _Ví dụ_: There is a new shopping centre near my house.
- **street vendor (n) /ˌstriːt ˈvendə(r)/**: người bán hàng rong.
  - _Ví dụ_: A street vendor was selling hot bread on the corner.
- **convenience store (n) /kənˈviːniəns stɔː(r)/**: cửa hàng tiện lợi.
  - _Ví dụ_: I bought some milk at the convenience store on my way home.
- **supermarket (n) /ˈsuːpəmɑːkɪt/**: siêu thị.
  - _Collocation_: do the shopping at a supermarket.
  - _Ví dụ_: My mother does the shopping at the supermarket every Sunday.
- **customer (n) /ˈkʌstəmə(r)/**: khách hàng.
  - _Collocation_: a regular customer.
  - _Ví dụ_: The shop was full of customers on Saturday morning.
- **bargain (v) /ˈbɑːɡən/**: mặc cả, trả giá.
  - _Collocation_: bargain over the price.
  - _Ví dụ_: You can bargain over the price at a traditional market.
- **discount (n) /ˈdɪskaʊnt/**: sự giảm giá.
  - _Collocation_: get a discount.
  - _Ví dụ_: I got a ten per cent discount on these shoes.
- **purchase (v) /ˈpɜːtʃəs/**: mua.
  - _Ví dụ_: She purchased a new schoolbag for her sister.
- **afford (v) /əˈfɔːd/**: đủ tiền mua.
  - _Collocation_: can afford something.
  - _Ví dụ_: I can't afford that phone; it is too expensive.
- **cash (n) /kæʃ/**: tiền mặt.
  - _Collocation_: pay in cash.
  - _Ví dụ_: My grandmother always pays in cash at the market.
- **queue (v) /kjuː/**: xếp hàng.
  - _Collocation_: queue up.
  - _Ví dụ_: We had to queue for twenty minutes to pay.
- **delivery (n) /dɪˈlɪvəri/**: sự giao hàng.
  - _Collocation_: free delivery.
  - _Ví dụ_: The shop offers free delivery for orders over 300,000 dong.
- **online shopping (phr) /ˌɒnlaɪn ˈʃɒpɪŋ/**: mua sắm trực tuyến.
  - _Ví dụ_: Online shopping saves a lot of time, but you cannot touch the goods.
- **window shopping (phr) /ˈwɪndəʊ ˌʃɒpɪŋ/**: đi ngắm đồ mà không mua.
  - _Collocation_: go window shopping.
  - _Ví dụ_: We went window shopping in the mall all afternoon.
- **shopaholic (n) /ˌʃɒpəˈhɒlɪk/**: người nghiện mua sắm.
  - _Ví dụ_: My cousin is a shopaholic; she buys clothes every week.
- **product (n) /ˈprɒdʌkt/**: sản phẩm.
  - _Collocation_: local products.
  - _Ví dụ_: This shop sells local products from my hometown.
- **price tag (n) /ˈpraɪs tæɡ/**: nhãn ghi giá.
  - _Ví dụ_: Look at the price tag before you buy anything.
- **refund (n) /ˈriːfʌnd/**: tiền hoàn lại.
  - _Collocation_: ask for a refund.
  - _Ví dụ_: The shirt was too small, so I asked for a refund.
- **souvenir (n) /ˌsuːvəˈnɪə(r)/**: quà lưu niệm.
  - _Collocation_: buy a souvenir.
  - _Ví dụ_: I bought a small souvenir for my friend in Hoi An.
- **shop assistant (n) /ˈʃɒp əˌsɪstənt/**: nhân viên bán hàng.
  - _Ví dụ_: The shop assistant helped me find the right size.
- **try on (phr) /ˌtraɪ ˈɒn/**: mặc thử, đi thử.
  - _Ví dụ_: Can I try on this jacket, please?
